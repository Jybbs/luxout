# Sensors

- [The *Outside* Sensor](./outside.md) says what the reading is, the range it stays inside, and where a sample day's reading spends its minutes.
- [Threshold Sensors](./thresholds.md) says how a sensor switches at a level, why it holds its state across a gap, and which automations suit it.
