# Troubleshooting

What do I do when a reading looks wrong? This section walks a reading that looks wrong from the limits of the estimate through debug logging and the debug line to a bug report.

- [The Limits of the Estimate](../estimate/limits.md) says where the reading can differ from the light you see, which covers most readings that look wrong.
- [Debug Logging](./debug-logging.md) turns on the line the plugin logs each minute, for *Luxout* alone or for all of Homebridge.
- [Reading the Debug Line](./debug-line.md) names each field of that line and where its figure comes from.
- [Reporting a Bug](./reporting.md) lists the fields the issue form takes.

[Privacy](../privacy/) comes before this section with what the plugin sends and keeps.
