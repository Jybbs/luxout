# Reporting a Bug

A reading that stays wrong after [the limits of the estimate](../estimate/limits.md) and [the debug line](./debug-line.md) is a bug to report through the [issue form](https://github.com/Jybbs/luxout/issues/new?template=bug.yml). The form takes the versions of `homebridge-luxout`, Homebridge, and Node, what you expected against what the reading showed, and a debug line from the minute it went wrong.
